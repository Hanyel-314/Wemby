About Wemby

Basketball Prodigy

Full Name: Victor Wembanyama

Date of Birth: January 4, 2004

Birthplace: Le Chesnay, France

Position: Center/Power Forward

Current Team: San Antonio Spurs (NBA)

Victor Wembanyama is a French professional basketball player, widely regarded as one of the most talented players of his generation. Known for his rare combination of size and skill, he was selected as the first overall pick by the San Antonio Spurs in the 2023 NBA Draft.

His unique skill set includes exceptional shooting ability, elite shot-blocking, and remarkable athleticism for his height. As a 7-foot-4 player, he displays unprecedented perimeter shooting and court vision, making him a revolutionary figure in modern basketball.
